The hook — pick one

Character counts are live. X allows 280 per post.

I made a shooter where your health bar is the magazine.
Every bullet costs your own health. A kill pays it back.
Miss, and the bullet ricochets off the wall and comes back for you.
-
Your ammo is your health.
Every shot you take is blood you don't get back — unless it hits.
Miss once, and the bullet bounces off the wall and returns to kill you.
-
Most shooters punish you with a death screen.
This one punishes you with debt: run out of health and you keep playing, below zero, until something catches up.
-
3 modes, 1 rule: your health is your ammo.
MARKSMAN — you aim, misses bounce back
AUTO-AIM — the gun aims, kills pay less
SIEGE — endless, can you withstand it?
